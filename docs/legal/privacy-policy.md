## 1. Summary

Cam Z is a camera app. It processes your photos on your iPhone. It does not send your photos, your location, or your usage data to us or to any third party. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking code.

## 2. What the app uses, and why

| Use | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | To show the preview, to take photos, and to analyse the preview for exposure, focus, framing and light so it can give advice. | Analysis happens on your device. Preview frames are not saved. |
| Photos (add only) | To save the photos you take to your photo library. Cam Z asks for add-only access and does not read your library. | Your photo library, on your device and in your iCloud Photos if you have it turned on. |
| Photos that Photos does not accept | If the library refuses a save, the photo is kept in the app's memory so you can retry it from the review screen. | Held in memory while the app is open. It is lost if you close the app. |
| Purchases | To sell the Cam Z Pro subscription. Apple takes payment and tells Cam Z whether you have an active subscription. | Apple handles payment. Cam Z keeps a record of your Pro access on your device, which lets Pro features keep working offline for up to 14 days. |

## 3. What we do not collect

We do not collect your photos, videos, location, contacts, identifiers for advertising, or usage analytics. Cam Z makes no network requests to send your photos anywhere. [CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLICATION: that no crash-reporting or other third-party service has been added to the release build.]

## 4. Apple and purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. We receive only what StoreKit tells us: which subscription you have and whether it is active. We do not receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID.

## 5. Children

Cam Z is not directed at children under [AGE]. We do not knowingly collect information from children. [CONFIRM the age rating and the App Store Connect answers before publication.]

## 6. Your choices

- You can turn off camera and Photos access at any time in Settings, under Cam Z.
- You can cancel a subscription in Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.
- You can delete the app, which removes the on-device Pro record and any photos still waiting in memory.

## 7. Security

Your photos stay on your device. Protection of the device itself, such as a passcode and iOS updates, is the main safeguard for them.
